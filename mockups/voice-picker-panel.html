Why this concept

  • Selection display is read-only — search lives inside the panel, so the current name never becomes the query.
  • Chip / dropdown filters keep the UI compact for Settings forms with several voice fields.
  • Favourites first, then provider groups — same information architecture as Concept A.
  • Closer to today’s autocomplete footprint; lighter than a full modal.
